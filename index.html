Before you start

  1. Strap the left controller on your left foot and the right on your right, on top of the shoe over the laces, buttons facing up. Velcro straps or controller ankle straps both work. Pull them tight, because any wobble shows up as tracking error.
  2. On the Quest, turn off automatic switching between hands and controllers (under the Movement tracking settings) so the headset doesn't drop the controllers mid-game.
  3. Clear about 2 × 3 m of floor. If you've done Space Setup, Dribble and Wall Pass use your real walls and furniture.
  4. Look slightly down while you play: the headset can only track controllers its cameras can see.
  5. You'll stand still for 3 seconds to calibrate: feet flat, toes forward, looking straight ahead.
