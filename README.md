# Volley XR

Mixed-reality football for Meta Quest 3, played in the Meta Quest Browser (WebXR).

**Play:** https://adamclayton1.github.io/volley-xr/

- Strap the left controller on your left boot and the right on your right (over the laces, buttons up).
- Choose **Feet** mode and your shoe size, press **Enter mixed reality**, then stand still for the 3-second calibration.
- Step on a floor pad to pick a game: Volley (with goalkeeper and defenders), Keepy-uppy, Dribble, Wall Pass.
- Look up at **◀ MENU** to leave a game.
